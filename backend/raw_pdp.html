Know Your Wear

Effortlessly elegant, this green embroidered kurta set for women is a perfect blend of style and comfort. The straight kurta features intricate floral embroidery on the hemline and sleeves, complemented by a round neckline with a keyhole detail. The three-quarter sleeves add a touch of sophistication, while the side slits ensure ease of movement. Paired with straight pants featuring an elasticated waistband, this set is designed for all-day comfort. The soft rayon fabric enhances the overall appeal, making it ideal for casual outings or festive gatherings. A must-have addition to your ethnic wardrobe, this kurta set is perfect for creating a chic and timeless look.

Styling Suggestion

Elevate your everyday ethnic style with this embroidered green kurta set. Pair it with minimalistic jewelry and flats for a polished yet comfortable look, perfect for casual outings or festive gatherings.
